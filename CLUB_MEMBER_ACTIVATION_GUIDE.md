# Activate your club's course app

Your sailing club provides access to the course app. You do not need to pay, create an account, or
provide an email address.

## Before you start

You need:

- the current version of the app;
- your club's invitation code;
- an internet connection for the first activation.

## Activate the app

1. Open the course app.
2. On the **Club access** screen, enter the invitation code supplied by your club.
3. Tap **Activate club**.
4. Wait for the course information screen to appear.

The app contacts the club licensing service and verifies a signed entitlement on your device. After
activation, already-installed course information remains available without a persistent internet
connection.

## Using the app offline

You can open already-installed course information while offline. The app does not contact the
licensing service on every launch. It checks again only when access is due for refresh, when checking
for future course updates, or when you choose Retry.

If the service or internet connection is temporarily unavailable, valid cached access is retained.
Airplane mode or a server outage is not treated as proof that your club is unlicensed.

## Messages you may see

**That club invitation cannot be used**

Ask your club for its current invitation. The old invitation may have expired or been replaced.

**Too many attempts**

Wait before trying again. Check that you are using the exact code supplied by your club.

**Update the app before activating**

Install the current version from the App Store and try again.

**Unable to contact the club licensing service**

Check your internet connection and try again. If you already activated successfully, downloaded
course information should remain available.

**Unable to confirm updates**

Downloaded course information remains available. Connect to the internet and tap **Retry** when
convenient.

**Club access has expired**

Previously downloaded reference information remains visible for safety, but it may no longer be
current and updates are disabled. Connect and refresh, or contact your club.

**Legacy access: bundled SYC course snapshot only**

An older SYC app unlock was preserved during upgrade. It covers the bundled SYC snapshot, not future
course packs or services. Use a current club invitation for modern access.

## Important sailing notice

Course information in the app may be outdated, especially when an access or update warning is
shown. Always check official sailing instructions, notices to competitors, notices to mariners,
charts, weather information, and race-day signals. The app is not a substitute for seamanship or
the skipper's judgment.

## Privacy

Activation uses a random installation identifier, club association, app/platform version, and
licensing timestamps. It does not require your name, email, phone number, Apple ID, contacts,
precise location, or sailing history.

If you need help, contact your sailing club and describe the message shown in the app. Do not send
the club your Apple ID, passwords, or private device information.
