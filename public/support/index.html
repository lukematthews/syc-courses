Quick troubleshooting

Location or bearing information is unavailable

  1. Check that Location Services are enabled for SYC Courses in device settings.
  2. Move outside or to a position with a clear view of the sky.
  3. Close and reopen the app, then select the course or mark again.

Actisense is not connecting

  1. Confirm the device is connected to the boat's Actisense Wi-Fi network.
  2. Check the host address, port, and TCP or UDP selection in the app.
  3. Allow local-network access when your device asks for permission.
  4. Use the app's connection test before starting an on-water session.

A course or mark appears incorrect

Stop relying on the displayed information and refer to the current official course booklet, sailing instructions, and race-day notices. When reporting the issue, include the course number or mark name and a screenshot if possible.

Before contacting support

  • Install the latest available version of SYC Courses.
  • Restart the app and your device.
  • Note the steps that reproduce the problem.
  • Do not email passwords, precise location history, or other sensitive information.

Reinstalling the app can remove locally saved settings, recent courses, and race-track data. Export anything you need to keep before reinstalling.

Safety
