## Background

A. The Licensor has designed and developed a software application currently known as **SYC Courses** (the **App**), which provides sailing course information and related functionality for sailors participating in racing conducted by or from Sandringham Yacht Club.

B. SYC wishes to obtain a perpetual licence for SYC and its members to use the App.

C. The Licensor wishes to retain ownership of the App, its source code, underlying technology, intellectual property and all rights to further develop, modify, commercialise and license the App and related technology to other persons and organisations.

D. In consideration for the licence granted under this Agreement, SYC has agreed to provide credits to the Licensor's SYC member account on the terms set out below.

## 1. Definitions

In this Agreement:

**App** means the software application currently known as SYC Courses, including the SYC-specific version of the application made available under this Agreement.

**Commencement Date** means [insert date].

**Credit Period** means the period of 36 consecutive months beginning on the Commencement Date.

**Intellectual Property Rights** means all present and future copyright, rights in software and source code, database rights, design rights, trade marks, confidential information, know-how and other intellectual property rights, whether registered or unregistered.

**Member Account** means the account maintained by SYC in the name of Luke Matthews to which membership fees, marina fees and other charges may be debited and credits may be applied.

**SYC Member** means a current member of Sandringham Yacht Club.

## 2. Grant of Licence

2.1 Subject to the terms of this Agreement, the Licensor grants to SYC a perpetual, non-exclusive, non-transferable and royalty-free licence to use and make the App available for use by SYC and SYC Members.

2.2 The licence is limited to the SYC-specific version of the App and its use in connection with sailing, racing and other activities conducted by or from Sandringham Yacht Club.

2.3 SYC may promote the availability of the App to SYC Members and may provide links or instructions enabling SYC Members to access or download the App.

2.4 Except with the prior written consent of the Licensor, SYC must not:

(a) sell, sublicense, assign, transfer or otherwise make the App available to another yacht club or third party;

(b) commercially exploit the App independently of the Licensor;

(c) represent that SYC owns the App or the Intellectual Property Rights in the App;

(d) copy, modify, reverse engineer, decompile or create derivative works from the App or its source code, except to the extent expressly permitted by law; or

(e) grant any other person rights in the App beyond the rights expressly granted under this Agreement.

## 3. Ownership and Intellectual Property

3.1 The Licensor retains all right, title and interest in and to:

(a) the App;

(b) the source code;

(c) all underlying software, frameworks, components, algorithms, calculations, data structures and technology;

(d) all general-purpose sailing, navigation, mapping, GPS, timing, starting, course, race-management and other functionality;

(e) all improvements, modifications, enhancements and derivative works developed by or for the Licensor; and

(f) all associated Intellectual Property Rights.

3.2 Nothing in this Agreement transfers ownership of the App, source code or any Intellectual Property Rights to SYC.

3.3 The Licensor retains the unrestricted right to:

(a) develop and modify the App;

(b) create other versions of the App;

(c) license or sell the App or related technology to other yacht clubs, sailing organisations, individuals or other parties;

(d) distribute software through the Apple App Store or any other distribution platform;

(e) charge users or organisations for access to other versions of the App; and

(f) use any general ideas, concepts, techniques, algorithms, functionality, knowledge or experience associated with the App in any other product or business.

3.4 SYC acknowledges that its licence is non-exclusive and that the Licensor may commercialise the App and related technology elsewhere without requiring SYC's consent or paying any royalty, revenue share or other amount to SYC.

## 4. Consideration and Member Account Credits

4.1 In consideration for the perpetual licence granted under this Agreement, SYC must apply a credit of **$695.00 per month** to the Member Account for each of the 36 consecutive months of the Credit Period.

4.2 The total credits to be provided under clause 4.1 are **$25,020.00**.

4.3 Each monthly credit must be applied to the Member Account no later than [insert day] of each calendar month.

4.4 Credits applied under this Agreement may be used against any fees, charges or other amounts payable by Luke Matthews to SYC through the Member Account, including, without limitation:

(a) marina pen or berth fees;

(b) membership fees; and

(c) any other charges ordinarily capable of being debited to the Member Account.

4.5 Credits applied under this Agreement:

(a) do not expire;

(b) accumulate to the extent they are not used;

(c) carry forward indefinitely until used or paid out in accordance with clause 4.6; and

(d) must not be forfeited because Luke Matthews ceases to rent a marina berth, changes boats, sells a boat, changes membership category or otherwise changes his use of SYC facilities, provided he remains entitled to maintain a Member Account with SYC.

4.6 At the conclusion of the Credit Period, Luke Matthews may elect, by written notice to SYC, either:

(a) to leave any unused credit balance on the Member Account, where it will remain available without expiry; or

(b) to receive payment of all or part of the unused credit balance in cash.

4.7 If Luke Matthews elects to receive payment under clause 4.6(b), SYC must pay the elected amount within 30 days of receiving written notice.

4.8 The obligation to provide the full $25,020.00 in credits is unconditional and is not reduced because:

(a) Luke Matthews does not own a boat;

(b) Luke Matthews does not rent a marina berth;

(c) Luke Matthews' marina or membership fees are less than the monthly credit;

(d) the App receives limited use or adoption; or

(e) SYC elects not to promote or actively use the App.

## 5. Distribution and App Store

5.1 Unless otherwise agreed in writing, the App will remain under the control of the Licensor and may be distributed through the Licensor's Apple Developer account, App Store Connect account or other distribution arrangements selected by the Licensor.

5.2 The Licensor retains control over:

(a) App Store listings;

(b) software releases and versioning;

(c) technical architecture;

(d) source code repositories;

(e) development and deployment infrastructure; and

(f) future commercialisation.

5.3 The perpetual licence granted to SYC does not create an obligation for the Licensor to maintain continuous availability of the App through any particular third-party platform where availability is affected by circumstances beyond the Licensor's reasonable control.

5.4 Nothing in this Agreement requires the Licensor to maintain compatibility with future versions of iOS, Apple hardware, App Store requirements or other third-party technologies without separate agreement and compensation.

## 6. Support, Maintenance and Development

6.1 Except as expressly provided in this Agreement, the licence does not include ongoing:

(a) technical support;

(b) software maintenance;

(c) compatibility updates;

(d) course-data updates;

(e) bug fixes;

(f) App Store administration;

(g) feature development; or

(h) other professional services.

6.2 The Licensor may, subject to availability, provide support, maintenance, updates, development or other professional services requested by SYC.

6.3 Any services provided under clause 6.2 will be charged separately at the Licensor's then-current professional hourly rate or at another rate agreed in writing between the parties before the relevant work is undertaken.

6.4 The Licensor is under no obligation to accept any request for support, maintenance or development work unless separately agreed in writing.

6.5 Unless otherwise agreed, the Licensor gives no guaranteed response time, resolution time or service level in relation to the App.

## 7. Initial Delivery and Defect Period

7.1 The Licensor will make the App available substantially in the form demonstrated to and accepted by SYC as at the Commencement Date.

7.2 For a period of 90 days from the Commencement Date, the Licensor will, without additional charge, use reasonable efforts to correct material reproducible software defects that prevent the existing functionality of the App from operating substantially as demonstrated.

7.3 The defect period in clause 7.2 does not include:

(a) new features or enhancements;

(b) changes requested by SYC;

(c) changes to course data or racing programs;

(d) issues caused by third-party platforms, services or APIs;

(e) changes to iOS, Apple hardware or App Store requirements occurring after the Commencement Date;

(f) issues caused by use contrary to reasonable instructions; or

(g) functionality that was not part of the App as demonstrated at the Commencement Date.

8.2 SYC grants the Licensor a perpetual, non-exclusive, royalty-free licence to use SYC course information and other materials supplied or authorised by SYC to the extent reasonably necessary to operate, maintain, demonstrate and support the SYC-specific version of the App.

## 11. Term and Perpetual Licence

11.1 This Agreement commences on the Commencement Date.

11.2 Subject to clause 11.3, the licence granted under clause 2 is perpetual.

11.3 The Licensor may terminate the licence only if SYC commits a material breach of this Agreement and fails to remedy that breach within 30 days after receiving written notice requiring it to do so.

11.4 Termination does not affect:

(a) accrued rights or obligations;

(b) any credits already applied to the Member Account;

(c) SYC's obligation to provide credits that became due before termination; or

(d) any provision intended by its nature to survive termination.

11.5 Clauses concerning ownership, intellectual property, confidentiality, liability and accrued financial rights survive termination.

## 12. Assignment

12.1 SYC must not assign, transfer, sublicense or otherwise dispose of its rights under this Agreement without the Licensor's prior written consent.

12.2 The Licensor may assign this Agreement and the App to a company or other entity controlled by the Licensor, or as part of a sale or transfer of the App or associated business, provided that the assignee assumes the Licensor's obligations under this Agreement.

12.3 Any sale or transfer of the App or associated business by the Licensor remains subject to SYC's perpetual licence under this Agreement.
